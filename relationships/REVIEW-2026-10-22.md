### Review standard

- A shared semantic tag and opposing norm kinds are **candidate signals**, not proof of contradiction. Ask whether satisfying the obligation *requires* the action the prohibition forbids. Here it does not.
- A `refines` edge implies a narrower or more specific requirement. Related work at different stages or boundaries may instead be `complements`.
- Preserve overlapping atoms when independently meaningful; a reviewed `complements` link does **not** deduplicate, weaken, merge, authorize, select, enforce or automatically prioritize either rule.
- All Tarium enrollment, externalization and publication actions still require the separate approvals and safety checks prescribed by the underlying rules.
- Reviewed edges belong in [`reviewed.json`](reviewed.json). The site and catalog represent these five pairs as reviewed links; the heuristic generator skips pairs with reviewed decisions. Additional future rules may produce new suggestions.
