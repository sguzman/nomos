# queues/specimens

Public-safe proposed rule and model specimens are placed here until explicitly authorized for analysis. Each atomic specimen remains independent, even when proposed as a member of a virtual grouping. Completed intake records remain here with pointers to their distinct processing records.

`SPEC-0013` through `SPEC-0066` are 54 independent development-hygiene rule specimens. `SPEC-0067` proposes first-class virtual rule groups. The eight separately identified group-definition proposals are in [groups/](groups/), including the umbrella collection and seven subgroups.

Group definitions are **queued model inputs only**. They have stable proposed identities and memberships, but have not changed the active ontology or public website. Queue membership authorizes neither specimen processing nor downstream task creation/execution.

See [WORKFLOW.md](../../WORKFLOW.md) for authorization boundaries.
