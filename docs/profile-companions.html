What does each rule profile cover?

Every existing rule profile has two source-grounded companions: situations where its selected rules may matter, and intentions they seek to serve. The original rules and profile approvals are unchanged.

These are descriptive snapshots, not active instructions or newly approved rule selections. Situation links do not restrict when a rule applies; intent links may describe methods, constraints, or composite ideas rather than atomic goals.
